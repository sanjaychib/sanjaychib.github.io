Where AI is now

Adoption is broad. Full scale use is still much less common.

88%

Using AI in at least one function

McKinsey reported that 88 percent of surveyed organisations were regularly using AI in at least one business function in 2025.

7%

Fully scaled

In the same survey, only 7 percent reported that AI had been fully scaled across the organisation. Another 31 percent were scaling.

20%

EU enterprises using AI

Eurostat reported that 20 percent of EU enterprises used AI technologies in 2025. Among large enterprises the share was 55 percent.

The useful question for managers: not whether AI exists in the organisation, but where it creates enough value to become part of normal work.
Sources: McKinsey Global Survey on the state of AI, 2025. Eurostat, Key figures on European business, 2026 edition. The surveys use different populations and definitions.
Shared case

Asteria Events

Asteria is a fictional premium hospitality group with hotels, event venues, restaurants and wellness operations. This exercise focuses on corporate event sales, not room bookings.

The commercial situation

Corporate event enquiries arrive through the website, email, sales partners and existing client relationships. Enquiries range from leadership retreats to product launches and multi day conferences.

Sales teams must understand the brief, check venue fit and availability, clarify missing information, prepare suitable options and build a proposal before the client can decide whether to continue.

Case data for the exercise

3,600 event enquiries each year

18 minutes average staff time to review and structure an enquiry

36 percent arrive without all information needed for a useful proposal

14 hours average time to a meaningful first response

28 percent of enquiries currently progress to a proposal

CHF 32,000 average value of an event that is won

Management question:
Where could AI improve the event sales process enough to create meaningful value without weakening pricing control, accuracy or client relationships?

Business rules for this case

Venue availability

Current availability must come from the live venue system. AI or staff should not estimate it from old information.

Incomplete or conflicting briefs

If important event information is missing or contradictory, the client must be asked to clarify before a proposal is completed.

Commercial authority

A sales manager may approve discounts up to 8 percent. A larger discount requires commercial director approval.

Step 2

Management has three different AI choices

All three are technically plausible. They are not equally valuable, easy or risky.

Option 1

AI enquiry assistant

Read incoming enquiries, extract the important details, identify missing information and prepare a concise brief for the sales team.

Option 2

AI proposal copilot

Use the client brief, venue information, pricing rules and previous proposals to prepare suitable options and a first proposal draft.

Option 3

AI sales agent

Respond directly to selected enquiries, prepare offers, follow up and negotiate within defined commercial limits.

Choose an option to examine what it could improve and what it would depend on.

Step 4

Run three situations through the workflow


Use the same Whimsical workflow from Step 3. For each situation, follow the route that the case should take. You do not need to create or edit another workflow.






Situation 1

Conflicting guest numbers


The brief says 180 guests in one place and 280 in another.


Route: clarify with the client before the proposal continues.






Situation 2

Venue unavailable


The live venue system shows that the preferred space is no longer available.


Route: use the live system result and move to another suitable option or sales review.






Situation 3

Discount request of 14 percent


The sales manager may approve only up to 8 percent.


Route: send the request to the commercial director for approval.








What Step 4 is showing:

AI can support the process, but some decisions depend on current system information, client clarification or human authority. The workflow should make those boundaries visible.
