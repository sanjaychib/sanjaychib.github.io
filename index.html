A small collection of interactive learning examples, showing how AI can support the design and development of practical, work-based learning and content. Each example starts from an everyday work task, asks the learner to make a decision, and gives feedback along the way.

Work changes› Capability gap› Behaviour defined› Practice› Feedback

Examples

Example 1

AI Capability Simulation

Can you trust the AI recommendation?

An AI assistant has prepared a customer case for review. The learner inspects the source evidence, checks what the AI got right or missed, and decides whether the case can move forward.

Skills practised: verify, question, decide, intervene

Example 2

Active Practice Simulation

Would you use this AI briefing?

The learner is a manager preparing a short update for a senior meeting. They check the evidence behind an AI-drafted briefing, judge its quality, and decide what to do next.

From training to practice

Open example
Example 3

Information Quality Check

Is this information reliable enough to use?

Before AI writes a case summary, the learner reviews four sources, from a policy record to employee notes, and decides for each one: use it, check it first, or do not use it.

Judging inputs before AI uses them
